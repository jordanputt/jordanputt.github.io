Privacy Policy

Personal Drive Sync · Effective October 26, 2026

Personal Drive Sync is Jordan Putt’s personal configuration of rclone for accessing their own Google Drive account. It is not a public service.

Data access and use

Depending on the permissions granted, rclone accesses Google Drive file names, metadata, and file contents to perform file operations requested by the owner, including transfers, backups, listing, uploads, updates, and deletions.

Storage and retention

Authorization tokens are stored in rclone’s configuration on the devices where it runs. File copies, caches, and logs may be stored on those devices or at storage destinations selected by the owner. These remain until removed by the owner or by configured retention settings.

Transfers and sharing

Google Drive data is transferred only to storage destinations selected by the owner to carry out requested operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Limited use

Personal Drive Sync’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke access through their Google Account connections settings and remove stored rclone credentials, file copies, caches, and logs from their devices and selected storage destinations. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing copies.

This website

This informational website does not request Google sign-in, collect Google Drive credentials, or include analytics or advertising scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement.
