PERSONAL RCLONE CONFIGURATION

Personal Drive Sync

Personal Drive Sync is Jordan Putt’s private configuration of rclone for transferring, backing up, and managing files in their own Google Drive account.

How it works

Google Drive access is used only to perform file operations requested by the owner. Depending on the permissions granted, this can include listing, downloading, uploading, updating, and deleting files.

This configuration is for personal use and is not offered as a public service. Google authorization connects rclone running on the owner’s devices to their Google Drive account.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for details about data access, storage, transfers, and revoking access.

This website provides information only. It does not sign users in or collect Google Drive credentials.
